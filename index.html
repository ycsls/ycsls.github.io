About

The Director

Yoann Casals is a French director, journalist and motion designer. His work combines documentary observation with cinematic aesthetics, creating films that privilege authenticity, patience and strong visual composition over spectacle.

He works close to the ground — with ranchers, craftsmen, touring musicians and the landscapes that shape them — trading spectacle for the kind of patience field reporting demands.
