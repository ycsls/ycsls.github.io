About

02 / 03

Yoann started out filing dispatches from places most editors wouldn't send a crew, and picked up a camera because the words alone weren't carrying the weight of what he saw. Fifteen years on, the reporting instinct hasn't left — it just found a wider set of tools.

He directs branded and corporate film alongside longer-form documentary work, much of it outdoors: ranch and rangeland stories, western horsemanship, working animals and the people around them. When a story needs a beat that live-action can't catch, he builds it frame by frame in motion design — including live visuals for touring musicians.

The throughline across all three: patience — waiting for the light, waiting for the source to say the true thing, waiting for the keyframe to feel inevitable instead of clever.
